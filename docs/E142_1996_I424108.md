# E142 helium-3 and neutron A1 campaign

`E142_1996_I424108` follows the repository's experiment/year/INSPIRE naming
convention. It is a custom, **UNVALIDATED** Rivet analysis for the 1996 E142
publication, not an official Rivet release analysis. Its event selection is
an explicit approximation for comparing theory with the published asymmetries.

## Measurement and reference data

- [Publication: hep-ex/9610007](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/9610007),
  Phys. Rev. D54 (1996) 6620–6650.
- [HEPData record ins424108](https://www.hepdata.net/record/ins424108).
- Helium-3: publication Table 7, [HEPData Table 1](https://doi.org/10.17182/hepdata.22340.v1/t1).
- Neutron: publication Table 9, [HEPData Table 3](https://doi.org/10.17182/hepdata.22340.v1/t3).

Only the **A1 columns** are compared. The neutron data were extracted from
helium-3; they are not measurements on a free-neutron target. No g1, A2, or
evolved-to-Q2=2 table is substituted. The complete mean x values are taken
from publication Tables 7 and 9 where HEPData omits them; the two targets
have slightly different mean x values. Reference values, statistical errors,
systematic errors and source checksums are retained in
`data/experimental/E142_1996_I424108/`.

## Approximate kinematic prescription

The incoming electron energy is **25.51 GeV** on a nucleon at rest. This is
one of the three experimental energies (19.42, 22.66, 25.51 GeV), chosen as
a representative setting with all published mean kinematics accessible.
It is not an inferred luminosity-weighted average of those settings.

The eight x bins are `[.03,.04,.06,.10,.15,.20,.30,.40,.60]`. Within each
bin the accepted Q2 range is centered on the corresponding published mean:

| x range | Mean Q2 [GeV²] | Nominal ±5% Q2 range [GeV²] |
|---|---:|---|
| .03–.04 | 1.1 | 1.045–1.155 |
| .04–.06 | 1.2 | 1.140–1.260 |
| .06–.10 | 1.8 | 1.710–1.890 |
| .10–.15 | 2.5 | 2.375–2.625 |
| .15–.20 | 3.1 | 2.945–3.255 |
| .20–.30 | 3.7 | 3.515–3.885 |
| .30–.40 | 4.4 | 4.180–4.620 |
| .40–.60 | 5.5 | 5.225–5.775 |

Three selections, `W025`, `W050` (nominal), and `W100`, use relative
half-widths 2.5%, 5%, and 10%. All require Q2 > 1 GeV² and W2 > 4 GeV².
The lowest `W100` window is therefore clipped from 0.99 to 1 GeV² at its
lower edge. x bins are half-open; x=0.60 is overflow, not the last bin.

The generator envelope is Q2 in [1,6.1] GeV², .01 < y < .95, W2 > 4 GeV².
It covers all three selections at 25.51 GeV. No original spectrometer angular
or scattered-electron-energy acceptance is imposed. x is integrated over
the published bin with MC cross-section weighting; this does **not** force
the MC mean x to equal the published mean, and it is not a pointwise result
at that mean. Accepted-kinematics histograms and per-x-bin `XMoment` and
`Q2Moment` inputs expose the generated weighting.

The weak dependence reported by E142 motivates this approximation; it does
not establish that a particular MC prediction is insensitive to the window.
Inspect the window shifts after generation. They test Q2-window sensitivity,
not the complete detector acceptance or x-averaging uncertainty. The region
near Q2=1 GeV² also approaches the chosen PDFs' lower scale boundary.

## Spin and target combinations

The descriptor uses the upstream per-output interface: each entry under
`outputs` contains its `target_components`, `observable`, `target`, and
`target_model`. The three `He3_*` outputs use the effective helium-3 mixture;
the three `Neutron_*` outputs select the free-neutron component. They reuse
the same generated proton/neutron histograms. The global
`combination.target_components` remains the helium-3 default for diagnostics.
Scale/PDF variations retain this metadata and only change histogram paths.
The E142/He3 combination tests are in
`scripts/tests/test_experimental_target_outputs_nbaldona.py`, avoiding a
filename collision with upstream's target-output tests.

This schema migration changes the descriptor signature. The postprocessor
automatically accepts the historical E142 schema only when the saved
`resolved-measurement.json` reproduces the original generation fingerprint
using the current source, card, reference and plot files, and transforming
that descriptor produces exactly the current configuration. Changed target
coefficients, output paths, cuts or other settings are refused. Comparison
families retain their separate signature check.

Reprocessing records both signatures and the compatibility proof in the
postprocess manifest section and summary. The original generation signature
and saved descriptor are preserved. Plotting old products under the migrated
schema is refused until postprocessing has recorded the current signature.
No event regeneration is needed for this verified schema-only change.

The nominal cards use `PowhegMEDISNCPol`, pure photon exchange, signed
`PositiveNLO` and `NegativeNLO` contributions, the real-emission spin vertex,
and shower spin correlations. PDFs are NNPDF40_nlo_pch_as_01180 and
NNPDFpol20_nlo_as_01180. Proton and neutron PDFs use the existing ThePEG
neutron isospin mapping, as in the COMPASS deuteron campaign.

For each target component the inherited convention is
`sigma_UU=(PP+PM+MP+MM)/4` and `sigma_LL=(PP+MM-PM-MP)/4`.
The ordinary, event-by-event 1/D-weighted, and covariance-proxy histograms
are combined only after generator cross-section normalization and signed-NLO
addition. The A1 prediction is `sigma_LL_over_D / sigma_UU`, rather than
dividing a bin-averaged Aparallel by D evaluated at the bin center.

D uses the electron formula with target-mass kinematics and Whitlow R1990
fit B. E142 uses the SLAC global R analysis (paper ref. 71); the fit choice
here is recorded explicitly. The extraction neglects eta*A2, consistent
with E142's central A1 extraction in Sec. 6.1. No separate A2/g2 prediction
or F2-based conversion to g1 is performed.

E142 Sec. 6.2 gives Pp=-0.027 and Pn=0.87. The per-nucleon helium-3 weights are:

| Channel | Proton coefficient | Neutron coefficient |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unpolarized | 2/3 | 1/3 |
| Longitudinal | 2 Pp/3 = -0.018 | Pn/3 = 0.29 |

Thus A1(He3) = `(2 Pp sigma_LL_over_D(p) + Pn sigma_LL_over_D(n)) / (2 sigma_UU(p) + sigma_UU(n))`.
The free-neutron output uses `(P,N)=(0,1)` in both channels. It receives no
additional nuclear-polarization factor. No binding, Fermi motion, off-shell,
shadowing or other nuclear corrections are modeled. The quoted Pp and Pn
uncertainties are recorded in the descriptor but are not added to MC
statistical uncertainties.

Helium-3 and neutron outputs share neutron events. The three windows are
nested and also share events. These outputs, and the two experimental
targets, must not be treated as independent measurements in a joint fit.

## Files and outputs

- Rivet: `analyses/rivet/dis/E142_1996_I424108.cc`, `.info`, `.plot`, `.yoda.gz`;
  `E142DIS.hh` contains the binning/windows and electron depolarization.
- Cards: `cards/experimental/E142_1996_I424108/` (one common card plus 16
  nominal cards), with 44 optional comparison cards in `comparisons/`.
- Registry: `config/experimental/E142_1996_I424108.json`.
- Runner: `scripts/run_E142_1996_I424108.py`, a thin wrapper around
  `run_experimental_campaign.py` that fixes the measurement and optionally
  redirects the output directory.

The two main postprocessed paths are `/E142_1996_I424108/d01-x01-y01`
(helium-3) and `/E142_1996_I424108/d03-x01-y01` (neutron). Four further
`A1_<target>_W025/W100` paths show window checks against copies of the same
reference data. They are not additional experimental measurements.
Additional Aparallel, helicity-closure, and accepted-kinematics plots are
diagnostics. The lower ratio panel is disabled by default.

## Running on the remote host

Run these commands from the root of the checkout you intend to use, and
replace `/path/to/work` with your results directory.

Activate the same polarized Herwig/Rivet environment used for COMPASS.
No GitHub push is needed. These commands use the remote host's local path,
not the mount path on the desktop computer.

First inspect a plan; this command generates no events and writes no campaign:

```bash
python3 scripts/run_E142_1996_I424108.py \
  --output-root /path/to/work \
  full --tag e142-pilot --jobs 16 --shards 10 \
  --posnlo-events 100000 --negnlo-events 10000 --dry-run
```

Remove `--dry-run` to run that pilot when ready. The existing `prepare`,
`campaign`, `postprocess`, and `plot` subcommands also work. `prepare` builds
the plugin and executes Herwig read for each logical card; `campaign` runs
the generated run files, and `full` performs all stages.

An example matching the event totals discussed for the COMPASS deuteron run is:

```bash
python3 scripts/run_E142_1996_I424108.py \
  --output-root /path/to/work \
  full --tag e142-production --jobs 192 --shards 100 \
  --posnlo-events 3000000 --negnlo-events 300000 --seed-base 424108
```

This example requests 16 logical runs × 100 shards = 1,600 jobs and
26.4 million generated events in total. Event options are totals **per
target component and helicity**, split across shards. `--jobs` caps concurrent
processes. Both targets and all three window selections reuse those events;
they do not multiply the job count. These totals are not a guarantee of the
same accepted MC uncertainty as COMPASS. The default descriptor uses a smaller
100,000/10,000-event pilot budget, not this production example.

`--comparisons` adds the existing unpolarized-NLO, polarized-LO, real-spin-off,
and shower-spin-off comparisons (60 logical runs including nominal).
The default, without that option, is already the full-spin polarized NLO+PS
prediction. The existing PDF-member and scale options are also supported.

Results go to `/path/to/work/E142_1996_I424108/<tag>/`.
Use the same `--output-root` and tag for subsequent commands. To inspect
the sensitivity after postprocessing:

```bash
python3 scripts/check_e142_window_sensitivity.py \
  /path/to/work/E142_1996_I424108/e142-pilot/postprocess/summary.csv
```

This reports changes relative to the 5% result, the individual MC statistical
errors, and shifts in units of the experimental total error. It deliberately
does not assign an independent-error significance to correlated window results.

## Validation

Python regression tests cover reference columns/errors/means, target weights,
job/card completeness, output paths, x-coordinate overlays, and the wrapper.
The compiled fixture check tests every x bin on both proton and neutron targets,
nested-window acceptance, the Q2 floor, wrong-lepton rejection, D against
the geometric paper formula, and the recorded ordinary/weighted covariance:

```bash
python3 -m unittest discover -s scripts/tests -p 'test_experimental_*.py'
python3 scripts/check_e142_rivet.py --output /tmp/e142-validation
```

These checks use controlled HepMC fixtures, not a Herwig production prediction.
They establish implementation behavior, not the physical adequacy of the
approximation. A generated pilot and examination of the window/kinematics
diagnostics remain necessary before drawing physics conclusions.
