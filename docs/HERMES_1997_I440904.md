# HERMES 1997 neutron A1 campaign

`HERMES_1997_I440904` is a custom, **UNVALIDATED** Rivet analysis for the
HERMES 1997 neutron result. It follows the approximate mean-Q2 prescription
used by the E142 and E154 campaigns, with the HERMES beam, binning and cuts.
It is not an official Rivet release analysis or a reconstruction of the
complete detector response. No production events accompany this addition.

## Reference data and provenance

- [Publication, hep-ex/9703005](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/9703005),
  Phys. Lett. B404 (1997) 383–389, Table I and Fig. 2(a).
- [INSPIRE 440904](https://inspirehep.net/literature/440904).
- [HEPData ins440904](https://www.hepdata.net/record/ins440904),
  [Table 1](https://doi.org/10.17182/hepdata.44586.v1/t1).

The selected quantity is **A1n**, the first dependent variable (`group=0`)
in HEPData Table 1. The g1 column and Table 2 integrals are not used. Values
are at each bin's measured mean Q2; no evolution to Q2=2.5 GeV2 is performed.
All nine central values, statistical errors and systematic errors were
checked against Table I. HEPData omits the third bin's mean x; the normalized
snapshot supplies 0.065 from the publication and records that source.

The exact downloaded source is
`data/experimental/HERMES_1997_I440904/raw-hepdata-table1.json` with SHA256
`018500455c15201f52fa5523389e85da6d194dfc598e5fb30686d34b74afd282`.
The normalized `reference.json` selects the observable, stores bin edges,
means, errors and provenance, and is checked against that source.
`scripts/phenomenology_reference_data.py` implements the selection and
writes the reference `.yoda.gz`. Campaigns use these local files offline;
`fetch-data` is a separate checksum-validated refresh operation.

## Beam, target and spin calculation

Use **27.5 GeV positrons**, PDG -11, on a neutron at rest. This differs from
the electron beams in E142/E154 and the 27.6 GeV beam in HERMES 2007.
The experiment used polarized helium-3, but its published neutron A1 has
already undergone the proton and nuclear corrections. Consequently this
campaign requires **neutron samples only**, with unit neutron coefficients
in both the unpolarized and longitudinal combinations. No A1(He3) output is
produced. The experimental effective nuclear polarizations, beam/target
polarization magnitudes and dilution must not be applied a second time.

The nominal cards use `PowhegMEDISNCPol`, pure photon exchange, separate
`PositiveNLO` and `NegativeNLO` contributions, the POWHEG real-emission spin
vertex and shower spin correlations. They retain the established PDFs
`NNPDF40_nlo_pch_as_01180` and `NNPDFpol20_nlo_as_01180` and ThePEG's neutron
isospin mapping. These PDF choices are campaign inputs, not inputs used by
the original experimental extraction. Each helicity sample is fully polarized.

The shared postprocessor first combines signed NLO contributions and the
independently normalized helicity samples, using the repository convention:

```
sigma_UU = (PP + PM + MP + MM)/4
sigma_LL = (PP + MM - PM - MP)/4
A1_MC    = sigma_LL_over_D / sigma_UU
```

The numerator uses event-by-event 1/D weighting. D includes target-mass
kinematics, neglects the positron mass and uses Whitlow R1990 fit B, the
implementation already used by the inclusive HERMES template. The paper
cites Whitlow (1990) as ref. 32. The covariance proxy retains sum(w^2/D)
needed for the correlated ordinary/weighted sums; MC errors are sampling
errors, not PDF or theory uncertainties.

The estimator drops eta*A2 in `Aparallel = D (A1 + eta*A2)`. Here the paper
also neglects A2 in its central-value extraction and propagates its
uncertainty. All published systematic errors are retained unchanged; there
is no second A2-error addition or new transverse-spin model. This differs
from E154's use of longitudinal and transverse measurements. There is no
g1 observable or fixed-Q2 evolution in this campaign.

## Kinematic prescription

| x range | Published mean x | Mean Q2 [GeV2] | Nominal Q2 interval [GeV2] |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| .023–.040 | .033 | 1.22 | 1.159–1.281 |
| .040–.055 | .047 | 1.47 | 1.3965–1.5435 |
| .055–.075 | .065 | 1.73 | 1.6435–1.8165 |
| .075–.100 | .087 | 1.99 | 1.8905–2.0895 |
| .100–.140 | .119 | 2.30 | 2.185–2.415 |
| .140–.200 | .168 | 2.65 | 2.5175–2.7825 |
| .200–.300 | .244 | 3.07 | 2.9165–3.2235 |
| .300–.400 | .342 | 3.86 | 3.667–4.053 |
| .400–.600 | .464 | 5.25 | 4.9875–5.5125 |

Every sample fills three nested selections with Q2 half-widths **2.5%**
(`W025`), **5%** (`W050`, nominal) and **10%** (`W100`). These are selected
comparison windows, not experimental Q2 bin edges. They do not require
three separate campaigns. All are intersected with:

- the published `Q2 > 1 GeV2`, `W2 > 4 GeV2`, `y < .85` cuts;
- the stated angular envelope `0.04 < theta < 0.22 rad`;
- physical `y > 0`, finite positive D, and `Q2 < 15 GeV2`, the published
  outer range (well above the largest selected Q2, 5.775 GeV2).

At Born fixed-target kinematics, `y = Q2/(2 M_n E x)`. Thus y < .85 clips
some of the first bin's low-x region. At Q2=1.22 it requires approximately
x > .0278, even though the bin begins at .023. The nominal window is an
intersection with the cuts, not a fully accepted rectangular x-Q2 region.
All nine published mean (x,Q2) pairs pass the selections. Their angles are
about 69–94 mrad. The widest window begins at Q2=1.098, above the DIS floor;
bins are half-open, with x=.6 excluded from the last bin.

The common card's generation envelope is `1 <= Q2 <= 15 GeV2`,
`.01 <= y <= .95`, `W2 >= 4 GeV2`; it covers all selected Born regions.
Rivet applies the tighter measured-kinematic cuts after event generation.
There is **no Eprime > 10 GeV cut**, which belonged to E154. Here y < .85
implies scattered-positron energy above 4.125 GeV in the target-rest frame;
the calorimeter's 3.5 GeV deposited-energy trigger is not separately turned
into a truth-particle cut.

The angular envelope alone does not reproduce the two detector halves'
azimuthal acceptance, efficiencies or the original x-y binning and
polarization/luminosity weighting. This implementation integrates over the
allowed x bin and narrow Q2 window with MC cross-section weights. The ratio
of integrated spin-dependent and spin-averaged cross sections remains an
x-dependent observable, with one value per bin; it is not the pointwise
prediction at the published mean x.

Inspect `UU_Accepted_*` distributions and the per-bin `UU_XMoment_*` and
`UU_Q2Moment_*` divided by the corresponding `SigmaUU_*` to check actual MC
means. Stability across windows tests this particular averaging choice;
it does not establish complete detector fidelity. The windows share events,
so their errors must not be treated as independent.

## Files and plots

- `analyses/rivet/dis/HERMES_1997_I440904.cc`, `.info`, `.plot`, `.yoda.gz`
  and `HERMES1997DIS.hh`: event selection, metadata, presentation and data.
- `cards/experimental/HERMES_1997_I440904/`: one common card, eight nominal
  helicity/contribution cards, and optional comparison cards.
- `config/experimental/HERMES_1997_I440904.json`: job defaults, reference,
  combinations, outputs and explicit physics assumptions.
- `scripts/run_HERMES_1997_I440904.py`: wrapper fixing the measurement and
  allowing a personal output root.

The main comparison is `/HERMES_1997_I440904/d01-x01-y01`: Table 1, first
x variable, first dependent variable. `A1_Neutron_W025` and
`A1_Neutron_W100` reuse the same experimental data for the two checks; they
are not independent datasets. Aparallel, helicity closure and kinematic
plots are diagnostics. RatioPlot is off by default; existing plot options
such as `--ratiolabel` remain available through the wrapper.

The reference YODA preserves x-bin edges for MC/data matching and records
published means as annotations. The campaign plotting step places data
markers at the published mean x values; optional data-component overlays
use the same coordinates. MC values remain averages over the configured bins. A1 plots use YMin=-.6 and YMax=.45 to
include all reference errors with the local YODA autoscaler. Expand the
range if an MC curve or its errors extend beyond it.

## Running on the remote host

Run these commands from the root of the checkout you intend to use, and
replace `/path/to/work` with your results directory.

Activate the installed polarized Herwig/Rivet environment. Use remote local
paths rather than the desktop mount path. First inspect this pilot plan:

```bash
python3 scripts/run_HERMES_1997_I440904.py \
  --output-root /path/to/work \
  full --tag hermes1997-pilot --jobs 16 --shards 10 \
  --posnlo-events 100000 --negnlo-events 10000 --dry-run
```

Remove `--dry-run` to run. This requests **80 shard jobs and 440,000 events**.
The dry run writes no campaign files and generates no events. `prepare`
builds the plugin and performs Herwig read, `campaign` generates events,
`postprocess` combines outputs, and `plot` renders the combined results;
`full` performs all stages. Each command requires its own matching tag.

An example matching the earlier per-target generation budget is:

```bash
python3 scripts/run_HERMES_1997_I440904.py \
  --output-root /path/to/work \
  full --tag hermes1997-production --jobs 192 --shards 100 \
  --posnlo-events 3000000 --negnlo-events 300000 --seed-base 440904
```

This gives 4 helicities x 2 NLO contributions x 100 shards = **800 jobs**
and **13.2 million generated events**. Event options are totals per helicity
and contribution, divided across shards. `--jobs` caps concurrent jobs.
The three windows do not multiply the event/job budget. These are suggested
budgets, not a reproduction of historical MC statistics or a guarantee of
precision. Defaults remain the smaller 100,000/10,000 totals with one shard.

`--comparisons` adds the unpolarized NLO, polarized LO, real-spin-off and
shower-spin-off families (30 logical runs including the eight nominal).
**The default already uses full-spin polarized NLO+PS.** Existing PDF and
scale-variation options also work. All results are stored under
`/path/to/work/HERMES_1997_I440904/<tag>/`.

After postprocessing:

```bash
python3 scripts/check_hermes1997_window_sensitivity.py \
  /path/to/work/HERMES_1997_I440904/hermes1997-pilot/postprocess/summary.csv
```

The 18 entries compare each alternate window to nominal in each bin. They
report the shift relative to the experimental total error and each MC
statistical error. They are not chi2 values, significance estimates or an
automatically derived systematic uncertainty.

## Validation

```bash
python3 -m unittest discover -s scripts/tests -p 'test_experimental_*.py'
python3 scripts/check_hermes1997_rivet.py --output /tmp/hermes1997-validation
```

Tests cover the reference values/errors/means, positron/neutron cards,
job budgets, target coefficients, YODA and postprocessing round trips,
window reports and a dry run with a personal output directory. Controlled
HepMC fixtures exercise every x bin, the windows, both sides of y=.85,
wrong beam/target rejection, x limits and 1/D covariance. A 5 GeV final
positron fixture guards against accidentally inheriting E154's energy cut.
Direct fiducial checks cover the Q2, W2, y and angular boundaries. The
independent geometric-epsilon formula checks the depolarization weights.

The implementation passed 75 experimental-campaign tests, six reference-data
tests and 20 compiled Rivet fixtures. All three A1 comparison plot scripts
were generated and rendered with synthetic prediction inputs.

These are software checks with artificial events. Herwig initialization
and production must still be tested in the remote runtime. Inspect a real
pilot's event statistics, signed contribution normalization, kinematics,
helicity closure and window stability before interpreting a physics result.
